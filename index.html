VankhaMods

TikTok LIVE gift mods for single-player games. Your viewers' gifts trigger effects in your game, and the popup shows who sent them.

How it works

  1. Download the VankhaMods Launcher and open it.
  2. Click Activate: log in with Patreon, or buy on lynk.id (QRIS, DANA, OVO, ShopeePay, bank transfer).
  3. Click Install next to your game. Your saves are backed up first.
  4. Set your triggers (gifts, follows, shares, likes, chat commands) in the launcher's Triggers tab, click Start, then connect your LIVE. No TikFinity needed (the webhook URLs below still work if you prefer TikFinity).
  5. Optional: OBS alert + goal overlays and sounds come from the launcher. Something wrong? Click Logs in the launcher and send us launcher.log.
